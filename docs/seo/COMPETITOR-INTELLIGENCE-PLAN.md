## Required Evidence

Every observation must record the query and query ID, geographic context, device, language, timestamp, source, collection method, competitor entity, rank or presence, page or listing URL, confidence, freshness, and limitations. A named competitor cannot enter the contract without a supporting observation.

Do not infer competitors from proximity, cuisine, a delivery-platform category, or brand familiarity. Do not record traffic, authority, rankings, ratings, or review counts without direct evidence.
