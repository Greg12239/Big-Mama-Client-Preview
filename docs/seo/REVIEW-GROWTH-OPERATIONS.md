# Review Growth Operations

Status: planning and human review only. The visible `4.8` text and three review cards are repository observations, not an independently verified aggregate rating. They remain excluded from schema previews.

## Request Channels

Receipt, packaging, counter QR, neutral staff requests, and an approved post-order message are candidate channels. None may activate until the official review destination, copy, placement, ownership, and measurement method are approved.

Ask every eligible customer consistently. Do not pre-screen sentiment, gate negative customers, condition incentives on feedback, buy reviews, use employee reviews, or create reviewer accounts.

## Staff Workflow

1. Verify the official review destination.
2. Train staff to use one neutral request without pressure.
3. Offer approved channels consistently to eligible customers.
4. Record aggregate request counts without reviewer identity.
5. Classify review topics and draft a factual response.
6. Require human approval before publication.

## Response Taxonomy

Use positive-specific, positive-general, service-recovery, food-quality, safety/legal, and spam/policy categories. Never disclose order or customer information in a public response.

Allergy, safety, threat, discrimination, legal, and serious health claims require immediate internal escalation. Move order-specific resolution to a private approved channel and do not confirm uninvestigated facts.

## Monthly Reporting

Future reporting may include aggregate requests sent, new reviews, responses, median response time, and topic counts. Live rating and review-count fields remain `null` until independently verified. No automatic response publication is authorized.
